# Privy BFCM Readiness Checklist

Source: BFCM_Readiness_Checklist.xlsx. Every workstream to check before BFCM. Reference these IDs (R01-R##) in the plan. The "Question to ask" line seeds the interview; skip any question the Shopify or Privy data already answers.

> **Plan window note:** the plan ends Fri Dec 4, 2026 (the Friday after Cyber Monday). Where an item below mentions December, January, holiday cutoffs, or gift guides, plan only the in-window part (for example the resume date for a paused flow) and list the rest under "Hand off to your holiday plan."


## Flows

### List Growth & Onboarding

#### R01 - Welcome Series (Email)
- **What it is:** Multi-step intro to the brand sent to anyone who joins the email list.
- **BFCM action:** Swap the evergreen welcome offer for a BFCM teaser or the live deal. Shorten delays so new subscribers see the offer before it ends. Add a final 'deals are live' step during the sale window.
- **Question to ask:** What's the current welcome offer, and should it switch to the BFCM deal or a teaser?

#### R02 - Welcome Series (SMS)
- **What it is:** Short SMS sequence confirming opt-in and delivering the signup incentive.
- **BFCM action:** Replace the standard incentive code with the BFCM offer so codes don't stack. Add a 'sale is live' SMS on launch day for anyone who joined in the last 30 days.
- **Question to ask:** Does the SMS signup incentive stack with the BFCM offer?

#### R03 - BFCM Early Access Signup
- **What it is:** Confirms the early-access signup and delivers the head start when deals go live.
- **BFCM action:** Net-new flow for the season. Build the confirmation, a 'tomorrow' reminder, and the early-access drop with a unique code or link.
- **Question to ask:** How far ahead of public launch does early access open, and what's the unique code?

#### R04 - Popup Coupon Reminder
- **What it is:** Follows up with visitors who claimed a popup or spin-to-win offer but have not purchased yet.
- **BFCM action:** Update copy and code to match the BFCM offer. Tighten the delay to a few hours since sale windows are short.
- **Question to ask:** Which popup offers trigger this flow, and are all of them changing for BFCM?

### Conversion & Recovery

#### R05 - Abandoned Cart
- **What it is:** Reminds shoppers to finish checkout, usually 2-3 touches over 24-48 hours.
- **BFCM action:** Shorten the first delay to 30-60 minutes. Remove or reduce the standalone cart discount while a sitewide sale runs. Add an SMS step and a 'sale ends' final touch.
- **Question to ask:** Does the cart flow include its own discount, and should it pause during the sitewide sale?

#### R06 - Browse Abandonment
- **What it is:** Nudges product viewers back with the item they looked at.
- **BFCM action:** Point out that the viewed category is included in BFCM deals. Increase frequency cap during the sale window; reset after Cyber Monday.
- **Question to ask:** Which categories are included in the BFCM deal, and can the flow reference them?

#### R07 - Back In Stock
- **What it is:** Notifies waitlisted shoppers the moment an item is available again.
- **BFCM action:** Mention BFCM pricing if the item is included. Send SMS first, then email; these shoppers move fast.
- **Question to ask:** Which waitlisted items will be restocked before or during BFCM?

### Post-Purchase

#### R08 - Order Confirmation / Thank You
- **What it is:** Confirms the order and sets delivery expectations.
- **BFCM action:** Add shipping timelines and holiday cutoffs. Suppress discount CTAs for 14 days post-purchase.
- **Question to ask:** What are this year's holiday shipping cutoffs by carrier and method?

#### R09 - Post-Purchase Cross-Sell
- **What it is:** Recommends complementary products after the first order.
- **BFCM action:** Move the delay earlier during BFCM so the recommendation arrives while deals are still live. Exclude items already in the order.
- **Question to ask:** Which products pair best with your top BFCM sellers?

#### R10 - Review Request
- **What it is:** Asks customers to leave a review.
- **BFCM action:** Pause or extend the delay during BFCM week to reduce inbox noise. Resume in December once orders have landed.
- **Question to ask:** When do most BFCM orders arrive, so review requests go out after delivery?

#### R11 - Replenishment
- **What it is:** Reminds customers to reorder before they run out.
- **BFCM action:** If a reorder window lands in BFCM, promote a stock-up bundle at the sale price instead of a single reorder.
- **Question to ask:** Which replenishable products could be offered as a stock-up bundle at sale pricing?

#### R12 - Post-BFCM New Customer Nurture
- **What it is:** Turns deal-driven first-time buyers into repeat customers.
- **BFCM action:** Net-new flow. Build now, goes live after Cyber Monday. Delay first message until after delivery.
- **Question to ask:** What should new BFCM buyers hear first: brand story, best sellers, or a second-purchase offer?

### Retention & Lifecycle

#### R13 - VIP Tier Entry
- **What it is:** Welcomes customers into the top tier.
- **BFCM action:** Grant BFCM early access automatically on entry. Make sure the threshold check runs before the Early Access drop.
- **Question to ask:** What's the VIP threshold, and does reaching it before BFCM grant early access automatically?

#### R14 - Win-Back
- **What it is:** Re-engages lapsed customers with an offer.
- **BFCM action:** Pause during the BFCM send window, or reroute lapsed customers into the BFCM sequence.
- **Question to ask:** Should lapsed customers get the BFCM offer, or stay paused until December?

#### R15 - Birthday / Anniversary
- **What it is:** Sends a personal offer on a birthday or signup anniversary.
- **BFCM action:** Pause for BFCM week or make sure the code doesn't stack with sale pricing.
- **Question to ask:** Do birthday codes stack with BFCM pricing, and should the flow pause that week?

## Popups & List Growth

### Signup Capture

#### R16 - Early Access Signup Form
- **What it is:** Signup form for shoppers who want first access to BFCM deals.
- **BFCM action:** Launch 2-3 weeks before Black Friday on the homepage and top collections. Promise a clear head start (e.g. 24 hours early) and connect it to the Early Access Signup flow.
- **Question to ask:** What's the mechanism for BFCM early access signup (email capture, SMS keyword, or both) and where does it live on-site?

#### R17 - Exit-Intent Popup
- **What it is:** Appears when a visitor is about to leave, giving them a reason to stay or sign up.
- **BFCM action:** Before launch, use it to capture early access signups instead of discounting. Once the sale is live, switch the offer to the BFCM deal.
- **Question to ask:** Which exit-intent popup(s) are currently live, and what offer do they show today — does it need to switch to the BFCM deal?

#### R18 - Spin-to-Win / Gamified Popup
- **What it is:** Game-style popup that awards a random discount or prize for signing up.
- **BFCM action:** Update prizes so no slot beats or stacks with the sitewide deal. Consider free shipping or a gift with purchase instead of extra discounts during the sale.
- **Question to ask:** Do we run a spin-to-win or similar gamified popup? If so, what prizes/codes need updating for BFCM?

### On-Site Messaging

#### R19 - Mobile Banner / Sticky Bar
- **What it is:** Slim banner that stays visible on mobile without covering the page.
- **BFCM action:** Refresh copy and CTA for BFCM and add a countdown to launch or end time. Check that it doesn't cover the checkout button.
- **Question to ask:** Is there a mobile-specific signup banner, and does its copy/CTA need a BFCM refresh?

#### R20 - Post-Purchase SMS Opt-In Display
- **What it is:** Thank-you page prompt asking new buyers to join your SMS list.
- **BFCM action:** Position SMS as the fastest way to hear about restocks and Cyber Monday drops. Skip the discount here during the sale window.
- **Question to ask:** Do we show any on-site displays post-purchase (e.g. SMS opt-in, upsell) that need BFCM-specific messaging?

### Targeting & Rules

#### R21 - Popup Frequency / Timing Rules
- **What it is:** Settings that control how often and how soon popups appear.
- **BFCM action:** Show signup popups sooner (5-10 seconds) before the sale, then limit to once per session during it so deal shoppers aren't slowed down.
- **Question to ask:** What are the current display frequency caps and delay settings — do these need adjusting for BFCM traffic volume?

#### R22 - New vs. Returning Visitor Targeting
- **What it is:** Targeting that shows different popups to first-time and repeat visitors.
- **BFCM action:** Show new visitors the signup offer. Show returning subscribers the live deal or a cart reminder instead of another signup ask.
- **Question to ask:** Are popups segmented by new vs. returning visitor, and should BFCM messaging differ between them?

## Campaigns

### Pre-Sale

#### R23 - Teaser Campaign
- **What it is:** Pre-sale email and SMS announcing that BFCM deals are coming.
- **BFCM action:** Send 5-7 days before launch. Hint at the offer, push early access signups, and share the exact launch date and time.
- **Question to ask:** When does the teaser/pre-sale email + SMS go out, and what's being teased (early access, doorbusters, sitewide %)?

#### R24 - VIP / Early Access Campaign
- **What it is:** Exclusive send giving VIPs and early access signups first shot at deals.
- **BFCM action:** Send 12-24 hours before public launch with a unique code or link. Suppress recipients who already bought from the public launch send.
- **Question to ask:** Which segment gets early access, how far ahead of public launch, and what's the unique offer or code?

### Live Sale

#### R25 - Launch Day Campaign
- **What it is:** The 'sale is live' announcement across email and SMS.
- **BFCM action:** Send at the exact go-live time, SMS first. Confirm the site banner, popups, and codes are live before the send goes out.
- **Question to ask:** What time (and time zone) does the sale go live, and is launch synced across email, SMS, and the on-site banner?

#### R26 - Mid-Sale Reminder
- **What it is:** A send during the sale to re-engage people who haven't opened or bought yet.
- **BFCM action:** Feature best sellers or a new angle rather than repeating the launch email. Exclude anyone who has already purchased.
- **Question to ask:** Do we send a mid-window reminder, and what changes in it (new products featured, urgency messaging)?

### Closing Push

#### R27 - Last-Chance / Final Hours
- **What it is:** Urgency send in the final hours before the sale ends.
- **BFCM action:** Send 3-6 hours before the end time with a countdown. Add a final-hour SMS that still falls inside quiet-hours rules.
- **Question to ask:** What's the exact end time of the sale, and what's the send cutoff for the final-hours message?

#### R28 - Extended Sale / Cyber Monday Push
- **What it is:** Messaging that carries the sale through Cyber Monday or extends it.
- **BFCM action:** Decide whether Cyber Monday continues the same offer or introduces a new one, and give it its own subject lines and creative.
- **Question to ask:** Are Black Friday and Cyber Monday one continuous sale or two distinct pushes with separate messaging?

## Segmentation & List Health

### Audience Setup

#### R29 - VIP / Early Access Segment
- **What it is:** Your highest-value customers plus anyone who signed up for early access.
- **BFCM action:** Build it now from spend, order count, or loyalty tier, and include early access signups. Refresh it the day before the early send.
- **Question to ask:** What criteria define the VIP segment (spend, order count, tenure), and is it built and current?

#### R30 - Suppression List
- **What it is:** Contacts who should be left out of BFCM sends.
- **BFCM action:** Exclude very recent purchasers from promo sends for a few days, plus bounced contacts, unsubscribes, and regions you can't ship to.
- **Question to ask:** Who should be suppressed from BFCM sends (recent purchasers, opted-out, restricted shipping regions)?

### List Hygiene

#### R31 - Sunset / Re-engagement Cleanup
- **What it is:** Re-engaging or removing subscribers who haven't opened or clicked in months.
- **BFCM action:** Run a re-engagement send in October, then suppress anyone still inactive (e.g. 180+ days) from BFCM sends to protect inbox placement.
- **Question to ask:** Should we sunset chronically unengaged subscribers before BFCM to protect deliverability, and by what inactivity window?

#### R32 - SMS Subscriber Compliance Check
- **What it is:** Confirming every SMS subscriber has documented consent to receive SMS.
- **BFCM action:** Audit how each subscriber was collected and remove anyone without clear opt-in before increasing send frequency.
- **Question to ask:** Are all SMS subscribers properly opted in with documented consent ahead of increased send frequency?

### Deliverability

#### R33 - Deliverability Warm-Up
- **What it is:** Gradually raising send volume so inbox providers trust your sending domain.
- **BFCM action:** Ramp volume to engaged segments through October and early November so the BFCM spike isn't a sudden jump. Confirm SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are set up.
- **Question to ask:** What's the sending reputation / warm-up plan for the BFCM volume spike (ESP throttling, IP/domain warm-up)?

## Site & Tech

### Performance

#### R34 - Checkout Load / Stress Test
- **What it is:** Testing that checkout holds up under peak BFCM traffic.
- **BFCM action:** Load-test or confirm your platform's capacity at several times normal traffic. Place test orders with every BFCM code applied.
- **Question to ask:** Has checkout been load-tested for expected BFCM traffic, and who owns that test?

#### R35 - Popup / Site Load Speed
- **What it is:** How fast pages load, including popup and app scripts.
- **BFCM action:** Test speed on mobile, remove unused apps and scripts, and compress sale banners and hero images before launch.
- **Question to ask:** What's current page load speed under peak conditions, and does the popup script add meaningful load time?

### Data & Tracking

#### R36 - Inventory Sync Accuracy
- **What it is:** Keeping stock levels matched between your store and marketing tools.
- **BFCM action:** Confirm real-time sync so flows and product blocks don't promote sold-out items. Pick fallback products for anything likely to sell out.
- **Question to ask:** Is inventory synced in real time between the store and Privy/ESP to avoid promoting out-of-stock items?

#### R37 - Tracking Pixels & UTM Audit
- **What it is:** Verifying analytics and ad tracking capture every visit and sale.
- **BFCM action:** Test pixels on sale pages and checkout, and add consistent UTMs to every BFCM email, SMS, and ad link.
- **Question to ask:** Are all tracking pixels (Meta, Google, etc.) and UTM parameters verified before the sale?

### Sale Pages

#### R38 - Landing Page / Sale Hub Readiness
- **What it is:** A dedicated page that collects every BFCM deal in one place.
- **BFCM action:** Build and QA it on mobile and desktop before the teaser goes out. Point every campaign, popup, and banner to it.
- **Question to ask:** Is a dedicated BFCM landing page or sale hub built, and does it need review before launch?

## Compliance & Deliverability

### Legal Requirements

#### R39 - SMS Quiet Hours / TCPA
- **What it is:** Rules limiting when you can SMS subscribers, based on their local time.
- **BFCM action:** Schedule pre-sale sends by recipient time zone. Send launch, sale-day, and deadline sends on the store's clock, and keep final-hour SMS inside allowed windows. Some states are stricter than federal rules, so confirm the ones you send to.
- **Question to ask:** Are SMS sends scheduled to comply with quiet-hours regulations even with a compressed BFCM calendar?

#### R40 - Unsubscribe & Preference Center
- **What it is:** The links and page subscribers use to opt out or change how often they hear from you.
- **BFCM action:** Test every unsubscribe link and confirm one-click unsubscribe is enabled. Offer a 'pause until December' option so overwhelmed shoppers don't leave for good.
- **Question to ask:** Is the unsubscribe/preference center tested and working ahead of increased send volume?

#### R41 - Legal / Promo Copy Review
- **What it is:** Reviewing discount claims and urgency language for accuracy.
- **BFCM action:** Make sure 'up to' and '% off' claims match real pricing, countdown timers reflect actual end times, and exclusions are clearly stated.
- **Question to ask:** Has legal reviewed discount claims, countdown timers, and "while supplies last" language for compliance?

### Inbox Placement

#### R42 - Sender Reputation Monitoring
- **What it is:** Watching bounce, spam complaint, and inbox placement signals during heavy sending.
- **BFCM action:** Check Google Postmaster Tools daily during BFCM week. Keep spam complaints under 0.3% (ideally under 0.1%) and pull back volume if they rise.
- **Question to ask:** Who's monitoring sender score/reputation during the high-volume BFCM window, and what's the escalation plan?

#### R43 - Spam / Inbox Placement Testing
- **What it is:** Checking that emails land in the inbox rather than spam.
- **BFCM action:** Seed-test key BFCM templates before the first big send. Review subject lines, image-to-text balance, and links.
- **Question to ask:** Are BFCM email templates being spam-tested before the first major send?

## Reporting & Post-BFCM

### Reporting

#### R44 - Real-Time Dashboard Setup
- **What it is:** A single view of revenue and key metrics while the sale is running.
- **BFCM action:** Set it up before launch with revenue, conversion rate, flow and campaign results, and popup signups. Assign someone to watch it.
- **Question to ask:** What metrics need real-time visibility during BFCM (revenue, flow performance, popup conversion), and who's watching them?

#### R45 - Day-Of vs. Week-After Reporting
- **What it is:** Quick daily check-ins during the sale plus a full review once it ends.
- **BFCM action:** Share a short daily snapshot during BFCM, then a full report 1-2 weeks later once returns and late conversions settle.
- **Question to ask:** What's the reporting cadence during and immediately after BFCM, and who receives it?

### Wrap-Up

#### R46 - Flow / Campaign Revert Plan
- **What it is:** A list of every BFCM change that needs to be undone after the sale.
- **BFCM action:** Log each change as you make it (delays, codes, paused flows, suppressions) with an owner and a revert date.
- **Question to ask:** Which BFCM adjustments need to be manually reverted after the sale (delays, discount codes, suppressions), and by when?

#### R47 - Post-BFCM Nurture Handoff
- **What it is:** Moving new BFCM customers into your regular lifecycle programs.
- **BFCM action:** Route first-time buyers into the Post-BFCM New Customer Nurture flow, then into standard post-purchase and win-back programs.
- **Question to ask:** How do new BFCM customers flow into standard lifecycle programs after the sale window closes?

#### R48 - Results Recap & Learnings
- **What it is:** A post-mortem on what worked and what to change next year.
- **BFCM action:** Compare results against last year's benchmarks, note what to repeat and what to cut, and save this checklist for next season.
- **Question to ask:** Who owns the post-mortem, and what past-year benchmarks should this year's results be compared against?
