# Privy BFCM Segmentation Checklist

Source: BFCM_Segmentation_Checklist.xlsx. 20 segments. Reference these IDs (S01-S20) in the plan. "How to build in Privy" gives the rule logic; replace placeholders (VIP dollar amount, high-spend cutoff, collection names, BFCM window dates) with the merchant's real values.

> **Plan window note:** the plan ends Fri Dec 4, 2026 (the Friday after Cyber Monday). Where an item below mentions December, January, holiday cutoffs, or gift guides, plan only the in-window part (for example the resume date for a paused flow) and list the rest under "Hand off to your holiday plan."


## Priority Audiences

### S01 - VIPs
- **Definition:** Your best customers: 3 or more lifetime orders, or lifetime spend at or above a dollar amount you choose.
- **Content / key messages:** Exclusive early access before the public launch. Lead with a thank-you, not a discount. Best offer or gift-with-purchase, free shipping waived. Tone: "You're first."
- **How to build in Privy:** Match any: Order count is greater than 2, or Lifetime spend is greater than or equal to your VIP dollar amount.

### S02 - BFCM Early Access
- **Definition:** Opted in for early deals this season through a signup form, campaign click, or keyword.
- **Content / key messages:** Deliver on the promise: 24-48 hour head start with a single clear CTA. Countdown to public launch, then a last-call reminder before deals go live for everyone.
- **How to build in Privy:** Signed up on your early-access display, clicked your early-access campaign, or has your early-access tag.

### S03 - Last BFCM New Customers
- **Definition:** Made their very first purchase during last year's BFCM window.
- **Content / key messages:** They only know you through a deal, so re-introduce the brand story. Showcase what's new since last year. Second-purchase incentive tied to BFCM.
- **How to build in Privy:** Match all: First order after last year's BFCM start and First order before last year's BFCM end.

### S04 - Last BFCM One-Time Buyers
- **Definition:** Bought during last year's BFCM and have not ordered since.
- **Content / key messages:** Win-back with a strong offer and "we've missed you" tone. Bundle around what they originally bought. Highlight improvements or new products.
- **How to build in Privy:** Match all: Last order after last year's BFCM start and Last order before last year's BFCM end.

### S05 - Engaged Non-Buyers
- **Definition:** Clicked an email or SMS in the last 60 days, but have never purchased.
- **Content / key messages:** Remove the barrier to the first order: reviews, social proof, product education, first-order offer. Position BFCM as the reason to finally buy.
- **How to build in Privy:** Match all: Clicked any campaign in the last 60 days and Order count is 0.

### S06 - New Subscribers 30D
- **Definition:** Joined the list in the last 30 days and have not bought yet.
- **Content / key messages:** Welcome-series style brand intro that builds toward BFCM. Set expectations for what's coming and when. Tease the offer without giving it away.
- **How to build in Privy:** Match all: Created in the last 30 days and Order count is 0.

## Spend & Frequency

### S07 - High rollers
- **Definition:** Recent, frequent customers whose lifetime spend is at or above your high-spend cutoff.
- **Content / key messages:** Treat like VIPs: early access, thank-you tone, best offer or gift-with-purchase. Do not train them to wait for a deeper discount.
- **How to build in Privy:** Match all: Last order in the last 90 days, Order count is greater than 2, and Lifetime spend is at or above your cutoff.

### S08 - Potential high rollers
- **Definition:** Recent customers with high lifetime spend who have not purchased often yet.
- **Content / key messages:** Invite a second purchase. Show complementary products and a path to becoming a regular. Strong but not desperate offer.
- **How to build in Privy:** Match all: Last order in the last 90 days, Order count is 2 or fewer, and Lifetime spend is at or above your cutoff.

### S09 - Brand enthusiasts
- **Definition:** Recent, frequent customers whose lifetime spend is below your high-spend cutoff.
- **Content / key messages:** Loyalty nod, bundles, and stock-up messaging to lift order value. Path toward VIP without leading on a steep discount.
- **How to build in Privy:** Match all: Last order in the last 90 days, Order count is greater than 2, and Lifetime spend is below your cutoff.

### S10 - Waiting for wows
- **Definition:** Recent customers who have not purchased often and whose lifetime spend is below your high-spend cutoff.
- **Content / key messages:** Lead with a clear wow: newness, social proof, or a time-bound BFCM reason to buy again.
- **How to build in Privy:** Match all: Last order in the last 90 days, Order count is 2 or fewer, and Lifetime spend is below your cutoff.

## Win-Back & Behavior

### S11 - Lapsed 90-365D
- **Definition:** Bought before, but nothing in the last 3–12 months.
- **Content / key messages:** "It's been a while" plus what's new. Strongest offer you're comfortable with. BFCM gives a natural, time-bound reason to return.
- **How to build in Privy:** Match all: Last order after 365 days ago and Last order before 90 days ago.

### S12 - Lapsed 365D+
- **Definition:** Haven't purchased in over a year.
- **Content / key messages:** One high-impact send with a bold offer. If no engagement, move to the sunset segment rather than continuing to send through BFCM.
- **How to build in Privy:** Match all: Last order before 365 days ago and Order count is greater than 0.

### S13 - Repeat Customers
- **Definition:** Two or more lifetime orders, but not yet in your VIP segment.
- **Content / key messages:** Loyalty nod and a path to VIP status. Cross-sell complementary products to past purchases. Bundle savings and stock-up messaging.
- **How to build in Privy:** Order count is greater than 1. Exclude your VIP segment when you send.

### S14 - Gift Shoppers
- **Definition:** Bought a gift card or a product from your gift collection.
- **Content / key messages:** Gift guides by recipient and price point. Gift wrap, gift notes, gift cards as a last-minute option. Ship-by deadlines front and center.
- **How to build in Privy:** Purchase history includes your gift card or gift collection.

### S15 - Active Subscribers
- **Definition:** Currently on an active subscription.
- **Content / key messages:** Exclude their core subscription product from discount messaging. Promote add-ons, one-time gift purchases, upgrades, or stock-up deals.
- **How to build in Privy:** If you use Recharge: subscriptions active count is greater than 0.

### S16 - Category: [Name]
- **Definition:** Purchased from a specific product collection. Create one segment per major category.
- **Content / key messages:** Category-specific hero products and deals with tailored imagery. Cross-sell the adjacent category. Repeat for each major category.
- **How to build in Privy:** Purchase history includes the collection. Duplicate this segment for each major category.

### S17 - Bought: [Item]
- **Definition:** Bought a specific product. Use this to cross-sell or upsell from what they already own.
- **Content / key messages:** Pair the purchased item with a complementary BFCM add-on. Use "your next order" language.
- **How to build in Privy:** Purchase history includes the product. Duplicate this segment for each hero product.

### S18 - Discount Shoppers
- **Definition:** Bought from your sale collection.
- **Content / key messages:** Lead with the offer: clear % or $ off, deadline, and urgency. Sitewide sale messaging works well here.
- **How to build in Privy:** Purchase history includes your sale collection.

## Suppression & Coordination

### S19 - Recent Buyers 7D
- **Definition:** Purchased in the last 7 days.
- **Content / key messages:** Recent buyers are good people to include in BFCM sends: they often buy again because complementary products are on sale. Leave them out of the launch email only, so the first "the sale is on" message doesn't land days after a full-price order.
- **How to build in Privy:** Last order in the last 7 days. Add this segment to Don't Send To on the launch email only.

### S20 - Unengaged 90D+
- **Definition:** Have not opened or clicked an email in the last 90 days.
- **Content / key messages:** One re-permission message before BFCM: "Still want our Black Friday deals?" No response means exclude from high-frequency sends to protect deliverability.
- **How to build in Privy:** Use the Unengaged Contacts smart segment, or: Did not open and Did not click in the last 90 days.
